ICAO Annexes

The International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) Annexes are the founadation of international aviation standards and recommended practices.


Annex 1: Personnel Licensing

Personnel licensing is the process by which authorized bodies approve individuals to perform specific roles, particularly in high-risk areas, by confirming they are qualified, skilled, and medically fit. This ensures that critical tasks are handled safely and reliably by competent professionals.

Annex 2: Rules of the Air

Global standards for safe flight operations, covering procedures and collision avoidance.

Annex 3: Meteorological Service for International Air Navigation

Meteorological Service provides essential weather data for safe and efficient international air navigation, aiding decision-making and air traffic management.

Annex 4: Aeronautical Charts & Amendments

Provide pilots with essential navigation information for flight planning and execution, available in various scales, including digital formats.

Annex 5: Units of Measurement & Amendments

Aviation uses standardized measurement units (SI and aviation-specific) for clear communication and safe operations.

Annex 6: Operation of Aircrafts & Amendments

Aircraft flight relies on aerodynamics, propulsion, and control systems, with support systems for safe and efficient operation.

Annex 7: Aircraft Nationality & Amendments

Aircraft are uniquely identified by nationality and registration marks, indicating their State of Registry using ITU-assigned prefixes.

Annex 8: Air Worthiness of Aircraft & Amendments

Airworthiness ensures an aircraft meets safety standards.

Annex 9: Facilitation & Amendments

Facilitation improves communication and decision-making to achieve shared goals.

Annex 10: Aeronautical telecommunications & Amendments

Aeronautical telecommunications link aircraft, ground stations, and satellites for safe air traffic management.

Annex 11: Air Traffic Services & Amendments

Air Traffic Services ensure safe and orderly aircraft movement and emergency response.

Annex 12: Search and Rescue & Amendments

Search and Rescue coordinates efforts to locate and assist people in distress.

Annex 13: Aircraft Accident and Incident Investigation & Amendments

The process analyzes accident causes to improve safety.

Annex 14: Aerodromes & Amendments

An aerodrome is a designated area for safe and efficient aircraft operations.

Annex 15: Aeronautical Information Services & Amendments

These services provide aeronautical information to enhance the safety and efficiency of air navigation.

Annex 16: Environmental Protection & Amendments

Environmental protection focuses on conserving resources and addressing climate change.

Annex 17: Security & Amendments

Air security protects civil aviation from unlawful acts.

Annex 18: The Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air & Amendments

Dangerous goods transport is controlled by strict regulations.

Annex 19: Safety Management & Amendments

A Safety Management System integrates risk management for safe aviation operations.
